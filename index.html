Beginner cardio session

Try this easy example, adjusting pace to your fitness and comfort.

Warm-up walk5 minutes
Brisk walk / cycle10–15 minutes
Easy pace3–5 minutes
Gentle cool-down5 minutes
Start at a pace where you can still speak in short sentences. Stop if you feel chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness.

Fuel your body

Simple Indian diet ideas

Build balanced meals with protein, vegetables or fruit, fibre-rich carbohydrates and healthy fats.

🥗🍲🍎

Balanced everyday plate

Roti or rice, dal or beans, seasonal vegetables, curd and salad.

🥗🍲🍎

Protein-focused meals

Eggs, milk, curd, paneer, tofu, dal, chana, rajma, fish or lean meat.

🥗🍲🍎

Smart snacks

Fruit, roasted chana, nuts in modest portions, plain yoghurt or sprouts.

Fat-loss nutrition

Use modest portions, prioritise protein and vegetables, limit sugary drinks and frequent fried snacks. A gradual, sustainable calorie deficit is generally more useful than crash diets.

Muscle-building nutrition

Eat enough overall food, include protein across meals, train progressively and allow recovery. Muscle gain takes consistency over months.

Sample day (flexible)

Breakfast: eggs or paneer with roti. Lunch: dal, roti/rice, sabzi and curd. Snack: fruit and roasted chana. Dinner: protein source, vegetables and roti.

Diet needs vary with age, health, activity, allergies and goals. This is general education, not a personalised medical diet. Consult a registered dietitian for medical conditions or special dietary needs.

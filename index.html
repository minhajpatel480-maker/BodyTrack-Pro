Biceps Curl

ArmsBeginner2–3 sets • 10–12 reps

Train the front of your upper arm with a controlled curl.

How to do it
  1. Stand tall with a light dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Keep elbows close to your sides.
  3. Curl slowly, then lower with control.

Strength training

Gym learning centre

Understand the major exercises, what they train and the key technique reminders.

🏋️

Chest Press

Targets chest, front shoulders and triceps. Keep wrists straight, shoulder blades supported and lower the weight under control.

🪽

Lat Pulldown

Trains the back. Pull the bar toward the upper chest without swinging or pulling behind the neck.

🦵

Leg Press

Works quadriceps and glutes. Keep your lower back supported and avoid locking your knees.

Example meal plan

Balanced everyday meals

BreakfastPoha with peas and curd, or eggs with whole-wheat roti.
LunchDal, seasonal sabzi, roti or rice, salad and curd.
SnackFruit with roasted chana or plain yoghurt.
DinnerPaneer, tofu, dal, fish or chicken with vegetables and roti.
Portions depend on age, body size, activity, health and goals. This is general education, not a personalised medical diet. Avoid crash diets.
